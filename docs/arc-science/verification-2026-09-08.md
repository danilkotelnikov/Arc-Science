## Open review finding — I1, Important

Repeated native cancellation can force-kill Python before its cleanup handler runs.
Because the renderer is in a separate session, native's group kill cannot reach it;
the renderer can remain active. This is a supported native cancellation path, not
merely a hostile child escape or an external crash.

The final fix safely handles ordinary cooperative SIGINT/SIGTERM, acquisition and
setup, with positive lifecycle and direct-child reaping evidence. It **does not**
close the repeated/force-cancellation gap. Do not rely on this build for
native-supervised rendering until that boundary is fixed and tested. Configuration,
offline BioArt and the other tests have narrower, separate evidence.

The independent whole-branch review raised I1 and unreliable descendant-lifetime
tests, plus four minor findings. After one combined fix wave, scoped re-review
accepted the stronger descendant/EOF/acquisition tests, artifact error/retry state,
caption corrections and generated-bundle packaging fix, but kept I1 open. The
selected Superpowers final-review process stops further fix waves here. The branch
and all review evidence are preserved; no merge or remote publication was made.
