# Code of conduct

This project is a place for people to build and use a tool together. These rules keep it a place where that is possible.

## What we expect

- Be polite. Keep arguments about the work.
- Assume the other person tried to help. Ask a question before you assume a mistake.
- Say what you know and what you guessed.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open. Do not post other people's private information.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain a term the first time you use it.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, threats, harassment, and discriminatory remarks.
- Posting private information about anyone.
- Spam and advertising that has nothing to do with the project.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem with someone's behavior, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or ask a maintainer in an issue
without private details. Security problems follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code of conduct applies in the repository, in the wiki, and in any space where someone speaks for the project.
